Dr Trushang & Vaishnavi — A Three-Day Celebration of Love

26 January 2023 | Mumbai

Some love stories feel like home. Dr Trushang and Vaishnavi’s wedding was exactly that—warm, joyful, and deeply emotional. Their three-day celebration in Mumbai was a beautiful journey of love, family, and togetherness, marking the beginning of a lifelong bond.

Day 1 – Sangeet: Joy in Every Beat

The festivities opened with a vibrant Sangeet filled with music, dance, and heartfelt performances. Surrounded by friends and family, Trushang and Vaishnavi shared quiet smiles and meaningful glances, radiating happiness. Every frame reflected celebration, pride, and pure joy.

Day 2 – Haldi: Intimate & Full of Colour

The Haldi ceremony was bright, playful, and filled with laughter. From candid moments to emotional blessings, the day felt effortless and genuine. An early morning couple shoot later captured the two in calm, natural light—simple, quiet moments that spoke volumes about their connection.

Day 3 – The Wedding: A Promise for Life

The wedding day was graceful and deeply moving. As rituals unfolded, emotions flowed naturally—tears of joy, heartfelt blessings, and smiles that said everything. Each moment, from the pheras to intimate family exchanges, was filled with meaning and love.
